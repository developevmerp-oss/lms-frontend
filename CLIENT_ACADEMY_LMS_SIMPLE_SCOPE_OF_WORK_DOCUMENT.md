## 🚀 1. The Big Picture: How Your Academy Runs in 4 Simple Steps

This platform provides your academy with a complete, automated business system. It attracts students, takes payments, teaches courses, reviews homework, and keeps students engaged every single day without you needing to do manual tech work.

- **Step 1: Attract Leads —** Students visit your modern website, register for your free webinar, and enter their WhatsApp number.
- **Step 2: Instant Sales & EMIs —** Students purchase starter courses or enroll in high-ticket masterclasses using flexible monthly installments (EMIs) via UPI or Cards.
- **Step 3: Teach & Mentor —** Students watch video lessons, attend live Zoom / Google Meet classes, and upload artwork photos for your personal feedback.
- **Step 4: Engage & Graduate —** Students build daily practice streaks, earn XP points, celebrate sales on the community wall, and receive verified digital certificates.

## 🔄 3. How Everything Connects (The Automated Ripple Effect)

The platform is engineered so that when something happens, the system automatically triggers all related actions without any manual effort:

| When This Happens... | The Platform Automatically Does All of This: |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Student Pays for a Course or 1st EMI** | 1. Razorpay verifies payment and transfers funds directly to your bank.<br>2. Student is automatically enrolled into all matching course levels.<br>3. Next installment due date is calculated and scheduled.<br>4. Celebration post is automatically added to the Community Win Wall.<br>5. In-app welcome notification is delivered to the student.<br>6. Meta Pixel sends a verified Purchase event to Facebook Ads. |
| **Admin Approves Homework Submission** | 1. Student receives custom XP points (+250 XP).<br>2. Progress bar advances towards their next level certification.<br>3. Leaderboard rankings update immediately.<br>4. Student receives a notification with your personalized critique. |
| **Student Attends Live Workshop** | 1. Admin marks Present on the class roster.<br>2. Student is automatically rewarded +100 Attendance XP.<br>3. Daily learning streak increments by +1. |
| **Student Logs a Real Art Sale** | 1. Order saved in their business ledger.<br>2. Student awarded +500 XP bonus.<br>3. Public community post celebrates their sale to motivate other students. |
