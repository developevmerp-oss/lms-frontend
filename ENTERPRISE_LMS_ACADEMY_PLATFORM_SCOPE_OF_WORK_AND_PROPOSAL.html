2. Student is automatically enrolled into all matching course levels.
3. Next installment due date is calculated and scheduled.
4. Celebration post is automatically added to the Community Win Wall.

4. Student receives a notification with your personalized critique.
Student Attends Live Workshop 1. Admin marks Present on the class roster.
2. Student is automatically rewarded +100 Attendance XP.

Student Logs a Real Art Sale 1. Order saved in their business ledger.
2. Student awarded +500 XP bonus.
3. Public community post celebrates their sale to motivate other students.
